# Product

## Register

product

## Users
SCPA is used by Indonesian students, fresh graduates, and early-career job seekers who need to compare real vacancies against their profile, skills, applications, and learning path. They are usually in a task flow: edit profile, inspect job detail, check skill gap, save or skip a job, and act on recommendations.

## Product Purpose
SCPA helps users find career-fit jobs using real job data, profile skills, semantic matching, interaction signals, and skill-gap guidance. Success means the app shows trustworthy job details, relevant recommendations, clear reasons, and practical missing-skill guidance without confusing loading states or fabricated catalog data.

## Brand Personality
Precise, restrained, evidence-based. The interface should feel like a reliable career operations tool: calm enough for long sessions, clear enough for comparison, and polished enough to make AI-assisted recommendations feel credible.

## Anti-references
Avoid decorative product UI that distracts from the job task, including custom cursor rings, stuck loader-like effects, broad glassmorphism, over-rounded cards, gradient text, fake sample job catalogs, and shallow one-line job detail pages. Avoid marketing-first layouts on authenticated product screens.

## Design Principles
- Show real evidence first: job details, required skills, source, and skill-gap data should be visible before decorative content.
- Keep task controls familiar: filters, autocomplete, save, skip, retry, and theme controls should behave like standard product UI.
- Separate loading, empty, canceled, timeout, and error states so users understand what happened.
- Preserve data signal quality: rich job descriptions and taxonomy-backed skill suggestions should feed the UI and model explanations.
- Prefer quiet consistency over visual novelty on product screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard navigation for forms and autocomplete, visible focus states, reduced motion preferences, and Indonesian plus English skill labels where useful.
